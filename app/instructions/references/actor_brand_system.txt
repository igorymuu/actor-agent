ACTOR BRAND SYSTEM

--------------------------------

ACTOR BRAND ELEMENTS

An actor brand consists of:

archetype  
energy  
casting niche  
visual identity  
public persona  

--------------------------------

ACTOR POSITIONING

Actors must occupy a clear position in casting perception.

Example:

Primary position:
young detective

Secondary position:
intellectual professional

--------------------------------

ACTOR IMAGE CONSISTENCY

Casting prefers actors with a consistent visual image.

Key elements:

hair  
style  
facial expression  
photo style  

--------------------------------

SOCIAL MEDIA BRANDING

Actors must maintain visual recognizability.

Recommended content mix:

40% acting
40% personality
20% industry

--------------------------------

ACTOR PROFILE STRUCTURE

Professional profile should include:

name  
city  
age range  
height  
type  
experience  
skills  
showreel  

--------------------------------

ELEVATOR PITCH STRUCTURE

Short introduction:

who you are  
what roles you play  
what makes you unique  

Example:

I am an actor with an intellectual professional type.
I often play detectives, analysts and doctors.
My strength is calm intensity and realism.

--------------------------------
