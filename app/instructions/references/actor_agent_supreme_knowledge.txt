CASTING DECISION LOGIC

Casting directors evaluate:

visual suitability
type clarity
reliability
role imagination
previous screen experience

Self tapes are often watched only for 5–15 seconds.

First seconds decide everything.

--------------------------------

SELF TAPE STANDARD

Frame: chest or medium shot

Neutral background

Even lighting

Clear sound

Actor must already be emotionally in the scene in the first seconds.

Avoid theatrical acting.

--------------------------------

SHOWREEL STRUCTURE

Length: 60–120 seconds

First 10 seconds must be strongest scene.

Show dialogue, emotion, conflict.

Avoid background roles and silent scenes.
